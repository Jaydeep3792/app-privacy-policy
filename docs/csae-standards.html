Zero Tolerance Policy: JUtility Studio strictly prohibits and condemns any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to maintaining zero tolerance for any activity that harms or endangers minors.

1. Policy Overview and Scope

This policy outlines the explicit standards and safeguards enforced by JUtility Studio, developed by Jaydeep Shrirang Sannake, to prevent and combat Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and the creation, storage, or dissemination of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), in full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, applicable international child protection laws, and human rights frameworks.

2. Strict Prohibitions

Users are strictly prohibited from using any feature or tool within JUtility Studio—including the Document & Receipt Scanner, Offline File Compressor, File Converter (JPG ⇄ PDF), Youtube Video Downloader, Insta Video Downloader, Loan EMI Calculator, Salary & Tax Planner, or PDF export/share features—to:

  • Capture, scan, create, convert, compress, download, store, process, export, or distribute any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Facilitate, promote, solicit, or encourage sexual abuse, grooming, predatory behavior, or sexual exploitation involving minors.
  • Attempt to use any on-device document, image, PDF, or media-handling utility to process, store, or transfer illicit media depicting child endangerment or abuse.

3. Application Overview & Safety-by-Design Architecture

JUtility Studio is a client-side Android personal productivity, file utility, and financial calculation application comprising seven core utilities:

  • Document & Receipt Scanner: CameraX scanning and Android Photo Picker import with interactive corner cropping, rotation, document filters, and multi-page PDF generation saved locally.
  • In-Device File Compressor: 100% offline compression for JPG, PNG, and PDF files with configurable presets saved locally to the user's Downloads directory.
  • File Converter (JPG ⇄ PDF): 100% offline conversion of JPG/JPEG images into A4 or Fit-Image PDF documents and extraction of PDF pages into JPG images.
  • Youtube Video & Insta Video Downloaders: Client-side personal media utilities that save publicly accessible video streams once into the user's local Downloads directory for offline playback.
  • Loan EMI Calculator & Salary & Tax Planner: Offline Indian Income Tax (Old vs. New Regime) and Reducing Balance Loan EMI calculations with optional local PDF statement generation.

No Social Hosting or User Content Feeds: All document scanning, file compression, format conversion, and financial calculations execute strictly on-device. JUtility Studio does not operate user accounts, social networks, public user profiles, user-to-user messaging or chat features, live-streaming feeds, or cloud/server file-hosting repositories. Downloaded media and exported documents remain solely on the user's own device.

4. Reporting and Cooperation with Law Enforcement

We actively cooperate with domestic and international child safety organizations and law enforcement agencies to eradicate child sexual exploitation:

  • Reporting to Authorities: If any violation involving CSAM, CSAE, or child endangerment connected to our software is brought to our attention, we review it immediately and report the incident to appropriate global authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement agencies.
  • Zero Tolerance Actions: Any attempt to abuse the application or its services for unlawful conduct will result in immediate termination of access/support and referral to legal authorities.
