Privacy Policy for JUtility Studio

Document scanning, file optimization, video downloads, and personal finance—unified in one studio.

Updated: November 22, 2026  |  Package ID: com.aistudio.takehometax.xkpzrl  |  Developer: Jaydeep Shrirang Sannake

Summary: JUtility Studio respects your privacy. Document scanning, image/PDF conversion, file compression, and financial calculations (Income Tax, Loan EMI) run entirely on your device. We do not sell, transmit, or store your personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how JUtility Studio ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Jaydeep Shrirang Sannake, handles information when you use our Android application. JUtility Studio is an on-device utility studio providing seven integrated tools: Document & Receipt Scanner, File Compressor, File Converter (JPG ⇄ PDF), Youtube Video Downloader, Insta Video Downloader, Loan EMI Calculator, and Salary & Tax Planner.

2. Information Collection and Processing

We believe in data minimization and user privacy by design:

  • Document Scanning & PDF Creation: Camera captures and gallery images selected in the Scanner are cropped, rotated, filtered, and exported to PDF strictly on your device using Android CameraX and local graphics APIs. No scanned document leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
  • File Compression & File Conversion: Files selected in the File Compressor (JPG, PNG, PDF) and File Converter (JPG/JPEG ⇄ PDF) are processed 100% offline on-device and saved locally to your Downloads directory without network uploads.
  • Financial and Calculation Inputs: Any figures you input (salary components, HRA, 80C/80D/NPS deductions, loan principals, interest rates, tenure, or optional names for PDF report headers) are processed locally in device memory and exported PDFs. This information is never transmitted to any server.
  • Downloaded Media: Video links entered in Youtube Video or Insta Video are fetched directly from public media endpoints and saved once to your device's Downloads folder for local playback. The app does not maintain server-side URL logs or user download histories.

3. Device Permissions Used

The app requests minimal permissions required strictly to perform its core functions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA, optional): Used only when you choose to scan a document or receipt with CameraX in the Scanner tab.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used strictly to check network connectivity and download video streams requested by the user in Youtube Video and Insta Video.
  • Photo Picker & Scoped Storage (Downloads): Image and PDF selection uses Android's zero-permission system Photo Picker and Document Picker (no broad media/storage read permissions are requested), and exported PDFs, converted files, compressed files, and downloaded videos are saved to your standard Downloads directory.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The application does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, data brokers, or user accounts/sign-in requirements.

5. Data Security

Because document scanning, file compression, format conversion, and financial calculations execute within your local Android device sandbox, your documents and financial numbers remain strictly under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

JUtility Studio does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general productivity and financial utility suite.

7. Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

We maintain an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Users are strictly prohibited from utilizing the app or any of its tools (including document scanning, compression, conversion, or video downloading) for the creation, processing, storage, or distribution of illicit or abusive content involving minors. For our full standards, law enforcement cooperation procedures, and reporting directives, please review our Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation Policy.
