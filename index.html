Privacy Policy for JUtility Studio

Effective Date: November 5, 2026  |  Package ID: com.aistudio.takehometax.xkpzrl

Summary: JUtility Studio respects your privacy. All financial calculations (Salary Tax, Loan EMI) and file compression tasks run entirely on your device. We do not sell, transmit, or store your personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how JUtility Studio ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Jaydeep Sannake, handles information. JUtility Studio provides on-device Salary Tax calculation, Loan EMI planning, offline file compression, and media utility features.

2. Information Collection and Processing

We practice privacy by design:

  • Financial and Calculation Inputs: Any figures you input (basic salary, allowances, HRA, 80C/80D deductions, loan amounts, interest rates) are computed locally on your device in real-time. This information is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • Files and Media: When using the In-Device File Compressor (JPG, PNG, PDF), files are processed strictly offline using local Android APIs. Your files never leave your device.
  • Downloaded Media: Media retrieved via the video downloader feature is saved directly to your device's public Downloads folder for your personal access. The app does not log URLs or download history on external servers.

3. Device Permissions Used

The app requests only standard permissions required for core functions:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to check connectivity and fetch user-requested media.
  • Storage / Downloads Access: Uses Android scoped storage APIs to save downloaded videos and compressed files directly into your standard Downloads directory. We do not read or scan any unrelated files.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The application does not use tracking SDKs, data brokers, or advertising profile trackers. Core utilities do not require account registration or cloud sign-in.

5. Data Security

Because all mathematical computations and compression algorithms run within your local Android device sandbox, your sensitive financial inputs and personal documents remain under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

JUtility Studio does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
