Privacy Commitment: JUtility Studio is built with on-device privacy at its core. Document scanning, image/PDF conversion, file compression, salary & income tax calculations, and loan EMI simulations execute strictly locally on your device. We do not sell, track, transmit, or store personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Overview and Core Features

JUtility Studio ("the App"), developed by Jaydeep Shrirang Sannake, is a client-side Android personal productivity and financial utility suite comprising seven everyday tools:

  • Document & Receipt Scanner: Scan receipts and documents with CameraX or import via Android Photo Picker, apply corner cropping, 90° rotation, Crisp Doc/Grayscale filters, and export single or multi-page A4/Fit PDFs locally.
  • In-Device File Compressor: 100% offline compression for JPG, PNG, and PDF files with configurable presets saved directly to your device's Downloads directory.
  • File Converter (JPG ⇄ PDF): 100% offline conversion of JPG/JPEG images into A4 or Fit-Image PDF documents and extraction of PDF pages into high-resolution JPG images.
  • Youtube Video & Insta Video Downloaders: Client-side personal media utilities that download publicly accessible video streams once into your local Downloads folder with direct playback and file-open actions.
  • Loan EMI Calculator: Reducing balance EMI computation, principal-vs-interest breakdown charts, full repayment schedules, and downloadable PDF statements.
  • Salary & Tax Planner: Indian Salary & Income Tax estimation comparing Old vs. New Tax Regimes (including 80C, 80D, NPS, and HRA exemptions) with itemized PDF report generation.

2. Data Collection and On-Device Processing

  • Document Scanning, Conversion & Compression: Captured images, selected photos, and PDFs remain strictly on your device. Processing uses local Android CameraX, graphics, and PDF APIs with zero cloud uploads.
  • Financial Calculations: All inputs (salary components, deductions, loan amounts, interest rates, borrower/taxpayer names entered for PDF headers) stay strictly within device memory and locally exported PDF files. No financial data is sent to any external server.
  • Media Downloads: Video links entered by the user are fetched directly from public media endpoints to save playable MP4 files once into the device's Downloads directory. The app does not maintain user accounts, download histories on servers, or tracking logs.

3. Permissions Used

  • CAMERA (Optional): Used strictly when you tap to capture documents inside the Scanner tab; camera frames are processed only locally on your device.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to resolve and download user-requested media streams in the Youtube Video and Insta Video tools and check connectivity.
  • Zero-Permission Photo Picker & Scoped Storage: Image and PDF selection uses Android's system Photo Picker and Document Picker without requesting broad media/storage read permissions, and saved outputs are written safely to your Downloads directory.

CSAE / CSAM Zero-Tolerance Declaration: JUtility Studio maintains an unconditional zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any use of our application to create, process, store, convert, download, or distribute illicit material involving minors is strictly forbidden.

4. Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

In compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, child protection laws, and human rights frameworks, we enforce the following strict standards (also detailed in csae-standards.html):

  • Strict Prohibition: Users may not use any feature of JUtility Studio (including the Scanner, File Compressor, File Converter, Youtube/Insta Video Downloaders, or PDF export tools) to create, capture, convert, compress, download, store, process, or distribute any form of CSAM or CSAE.
  • Safety-by-Design Architecture: JUtility Studio operates purely on-device and does not host user accounts, public profiles, user-to-user messaging, chat forums, or public file-sharing servers.
  • No Facilitation: Using any tool within the app to promote, encourage, or facilitate predatory behavior, grooming, or child exploitation is strictly prohibited and illegal.
  • Reporting to Authorities: We actively cooperate with child safety organizations and law enforcement. Any credible report of CSAM or child endangerment is immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant international law enforcement agencies.
