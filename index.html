Privacy Policy & Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Application: JUtility Studio  |  Package: com.aistudio.takehometax.xkpzrl  |  Developer: Jaydeep Sannake

Privacy Commitment: All financial calculations (Salary Tax, Loan EMI) and file compression tasks run entirely on your device. We do not sell, track, transmit, or store personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Overview and Core Purpose

JUtility Studio ("the App"), developed by Jaydeep Sannake, provides on-device utility features including Indian Salary & Income Tax estimation (Old vs. New Tax Regimes with Budget 2024-25/2025-26 rules), Loan EMI calculations with repayment schedules, in-device offline file compression (JPG, PNG, PDF), and personal media download utilities.

2. Data Collection and Processing

  • Financial Calculations: All figures (salary, allowances, 80C/80D deductions, loan amounts) are processed locally in real-time. No financial figures are transmitted to any cloud servers.
  • Local File Compression: Files selected for compression remain strictly on-device. Processing uses Android's local graphics and PDF renderers without external network transfers.
  • Media Downloads: Downloaded media requested by the user is stored directly in the device's public Downloads folder. The app does not log or track your downloaded media or URLs.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to fetch user-requested video media and verify network connectivity.
  • Scoped Storage (Downloads): Used to write your exported reports and downloaded media files into your standard Downloads directory.

Zero-Tolerance CSAE / CSAM Policy: JUtility Studio maintains an unconditional zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any use of our application to handle or facilitate illicit material involving minors is strictly forbidden.
