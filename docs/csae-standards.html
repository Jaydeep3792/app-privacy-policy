Safety Policy & Compliance Standards

Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Application: JUtility Studio  |  Package: com.aistudio.takehometax.xkpzrl  |  Developer: Jaydeep Sannake

Zero Tolerance Policy: JUtility Studio strictly prohibits and condemns any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We maintain zero tolerance for any activity that harms or endangers minors.

1. Policy Overview and Scope

This policy outlines the explicit standards enforced by JUtility Studio to prevent and combat Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), in full compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies and international child protection frameworks.

2. Strict Prohibitions

Users are strictly prohibited from utilizing any feature of JUtility Studio—including file compression or media utility tools—to:

  • Create, compress, store, process, transfer, or distribute any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Facilitate, promote, or encourage predatory behavior, grooming, or sexual exploitation involving minors.
  • Attempt to use utility features to process or handle illicit media depicting child endangerment.

3. Nature of the Application

JUtility Studio is an on-device utility application designed for financial calculations (Salary Tax and Loan EMI planning) and client-side device utilities (offline file compression and media utilities). The application does not operate social networks, public user profiles, user-to-user messaging forums, or public hosting repositories.

4. Reporting and Law Enforcement Cooperation

We actively cooperate with child safety organizations and law enforcement agencies to eradicate child sexual exploitation:

  • Reporting to Authorities: If any violation involving CSAM or child endangerment is brought to our attention, we immediately report the matter to appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement bodies.
  • Zero Tolerance Actions: Any attempt to abuse the app's services for unlawful conduct will result in immediate termination of access and reporting to legal authorities.

5. How to Report Violations

If you discover or suspect any violation of our CSAE standards connected to our software, report it immediately to our safety contact:

  • Safety & Compliance Officer: Jaydeep Sannake
  • Email: Jaydeep.Sannake@gmail.com
  • Direct Law Enforcement Reporting: You can also report directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children at report.cybertip.org.
